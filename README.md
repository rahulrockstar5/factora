# 🔍 Factora
 
> 📰 Paste a social media link and get a 0 to 100 score for how likely the news is to be real.
 
---
 
## ✨ Features
 
- 🔗 Link box with a **Check news** button
- 🎨 Three themes: Colorful (default), Dark and Light
- 📡 Animated radar scan while the post is checked
- 💯 Score out of 100 with a real/fake split and confidence level
- 🏛️ Cross-check against official accounts, government bodies, news agencies, fact-checkers and research
- 🚩 Warning signs such as lookalike domains, shortened links and hoax-style wording
 
---
 
## ⚙️ How it works
 
The link is parsed and checked against lists of trusted domains and accounts, and any post text you add is scanned for hoax patterns. When available, Claude analyses the link and text and gives a verdict for each source type. Otherwise the page uses the built-in checks only.
 
---
 
## ⚠️ Limits
 
- 🌐 It cannot open posts or search the live web. With only a link the score stays near the middle, so paste the post text for a better result.
- 📊 The score is an estimate, not a fact-checker's ruling.
 
---
 
## 📢 Disclaimer
 
Factora is a study project. It can be wrong. Do not use its score as the only basis for sharing, reporting or acting on a piece of news.
