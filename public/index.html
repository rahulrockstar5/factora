Factora

Social post link checker

Is that post real? Paste the link and find out.

Factora reads the post, picks out the claim and compares it with what official accounts, government bodies, news agencies and fact-checkers have said. You get a score out of 100.

How it works

  1. Paste the linkAny public post, or a news page that is being shared around.
  2. Watch the scanFactora reads the link, finds the claim and weighs it against five kinds of sources.
  3. Read the scoreYou get a number out of 100 and the reasons behind it.
